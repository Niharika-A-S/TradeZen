## SEBI Study on FY22 Derivatives Trading
A SEBI study found 89% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY22, with an average loss of about ₹1.1 lakh.

## SEBI Study Across FY22 to FY24
A SEBI study covering FY22 to FY24 reported about 93% of individual F&O traders lost money.

## Net Losses in Derivatives for FY25
For FY25, over 91% of individual F&O traders made losses, with reported net losses of ₹1,05,603 crore across the industry.

## Intraday Cash Segment Realities
In the intraday equity cash segment, 71% of individual traders lost money in FY23, and 80% among those who made more than 500 trades a year.

## Purpose of Market Statistics
These figures describe real-money traders in specific market segments. They give context and do not predict any one user.

## Regulatory Scope and Role of TradeZen Coach
Giving investment advice in India requires SEBI registration. TradeZen Coach educates, uses paper trading only, and does not give stock tips or predictions.
