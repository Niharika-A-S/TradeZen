## What it looks like
Trading again soon after a loss, often with a bigger size, to win the money back. Example: a 10-share trade loses ₹900, and minutes later the next trade uses 20 shares.

## Why it happens
Many traders find a loss uncomfortable and want to get even quickly. That urge pushes decisions faster and bigger than the plan allows. It is a very human reaction, not a character flaw.

## What the numbers say
Doubling the size after every loss: 1,000 + 2,000 + 4,000 + 8,000 = ₹15,000 lost after four losses in a row, against ₹4,000 at a constant size.

## A gentler habit
Add a pause after a loss, for example ten minutes away from the screen, and keep the next trade at your normal size. Write the pause into your rules so the plan decides, not the mood.
