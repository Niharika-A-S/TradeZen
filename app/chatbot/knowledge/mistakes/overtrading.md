## What it looks like
Placing many more trades than planned, often entering low-conviction setups simply to stay active in the market. Example: executing 14 trades in an hour when your rule limit is 4 trades per day.

## Why it happens
Many traders experience boredom or fear of missing out when sitting on hands during quiet market periods. The excitement of market action tricks the brain into feeling that more activity equals higher profit.

## What the numbers say
Taking 12 extra trades a day with an average brokerage and slippage cost of ₹50 per trade adds ₹600 per day, or ₹12,000 every month just in friction costs.

## A gentler habit
Set a hard daily trade limit in your TradeZen rules, such as 3 trades per day, and shut down your trading platform once your limit is reached.
