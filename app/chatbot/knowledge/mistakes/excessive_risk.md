## What it looks like
Allocating a massive percentage of your account into a single speculative trade. Example: risking 10% or ₹1,00,000 of a ₹10,00,000 account on one intraday position.

## Why it happens
Many traders overestimate their probability of winning and seek quick financial windfalls, underestimating the statistical likelihood of consecutive losses.

## What the numbers say
A 50% account drawdown requires a 100% gain just to break even, while limiting risk to 1% per trade allows 20 consecutive losses with over 80% capital preserved.

## A gentler habit
Cap your risk per trade at a fixed 1% to 2% of total capital regardless of how attractive a chart appears.
