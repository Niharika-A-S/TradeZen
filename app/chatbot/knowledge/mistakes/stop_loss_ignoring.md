## What it looks like
Entering orders without setting a stop-loss or cancelling an active stop-loss when price moves against you. Example: buying at ₹500, removing the stop-loss at ₹490, and watching the stock decline to ₹420.

## Why it happens
Many traders hate accepting a small loss because it feels like an admission of being wrong. Hope replaces strategy in the wish that price will bounce back to break-even.

## What the numbers say
Allowing one uncontrolled loss of ₹8,000 erases the profits of 8 disciplined winning trades that made ₹1,000 each.

## A gentler habit
Treat stop-losses as non-negotiable insurance costs of doing business, and place the stop order simultaneously with your trade entry.
