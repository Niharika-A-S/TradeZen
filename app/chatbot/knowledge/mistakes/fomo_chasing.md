## What it looks like
Jumping into a stock after it has already rallied multiple percent in minutes, fearing you are missing an easy win. Example: buying at the peak of a ₹40 rapid candle after 14:30.

## Why it happens
Many traders feel intense envy when seeing green candles on social feeds or screeners, prompting impulsive market buys at unfavourable risk-reward prices.

## What the numbers say
Buying at the late extension of an intraday run experiences an adverse reversal on 7 out of 10 occasions, risking ₹1,500 for an average bounce of only ₹300.

## A gentler habit
Wait for a verified pullback and base before entering, and accept that missing a move is far cheaper than catching the top.
